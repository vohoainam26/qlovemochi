# QLove Full Website Client Feedback Guide

Welcome to the QLove website review process! To ensure your feedback is clearly understood and quickly implemented, please use the provided QLove_Full_Website_Client_Feedback.xlsx workbook (or the uploaded Google Sheet if configured) to review the website.

## What to Check

For every area listed in the "Review Map", please check:

1. **Visual hierarchy / layout**: Is the content organized logically?
2. **Product size**: Are the products displayed at an appropriate scale?
3. **Animation / motion**: Do the entry animations and background motions feel right?
4. **Transition / flow**: Are the transitions between sections smooth?
5. **Hover / click / navigation**: Do interactive elements behave as expected?
6. **Copy / text**: Is the text accurate, readable, and free of typos?
7. **Mobile / responsive**: Does the site look good and function well on phones and tablets?

## How to Write Feedback

When writing feedback in the spreadsheet or issue log, please be as specific as possible.

**Ask yourself to write:**
`	ext
What I see now:
...

What I want changed:
...

Priority:
High / Medium / Low
`

**Avoid vague comments such as:**
> "I don't like this."

**Prefer specific, actionable comments such as:**
> "The products in QLove Pouch Mix feel too small compared with the title. Please enlarge the pouch group while keeping the existing moving-shelf concept. Priority: High."

## Privacy Note
The feedback sheet is a **private handoff tool**. Do not publish or expose the link in the production website source or navigation.
